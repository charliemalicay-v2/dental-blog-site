Discomfort is information. Sometimes it is minor and can wait for your next
scheduled visit. Other times it is a signal to act quickly. Here are seven signs
worth a call this week — and two that mean you should not wait at all.

## Call now, not later

**Facial swelling with fever or trouble swallowing or breathing.** Swelling that
spreads into your face or neck can indicate a spreading infection. This is a
medical emergency. Seek urgent care or call 911.

**Trauma to a tooth or jaw.** A knocked-out, broken, or displaced tooth has the
best chance of being saved when treated quickly. Keep a knocked-out tooth moist
and get seen immediately.

## Call this week

**1. Pain that wakes you up.** A toothache that disrupts sleep is usually not
nothing. It can point to decay reaching the nerve, a cracked tooth, or an
infection.

**2. Temperature sensitivity that lingers.** A quick twinge from cold is common.
Pain that continues after the cold is gone deserves a look.

**3. A cracked or chipped tooth.** Even if it does not hurt right now, an
exposed edge invites decay and can worsen under biting pressure.

**4. Bleeding gums when brushing.** Occasional irritation happens, but gums that
bleed regularly are a hallmark of early gum disease, when it is most treatable.

**5. A persistent sore or lump.** Any sore in the mouth that does not resolve
within about two weeks should be evaluated.

**6. A bad taste, odor, or discharge.** These can signal an infection or an
abscess that needs treatment.

**7. A tooth that feels loose or "hits first."** A change in how your bite
closes can reflect a shifting tooth, a crack, or an infection at the root.

## What to do while you wait

Keep the area clean, avoid very hot, cold, or sweet foods, and use over-the-
counter pain relief as directed if you can take it safely. Do not place aspirin
directly on the gum — it can burn the tissue.

If you are unsure whether something is urgent, call the office and describe
what you are feeling. Front-desk and clinical staff triage this every day and
will tell you whether to come in now or soon.
